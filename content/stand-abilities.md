# Stand Abilities

Source of the compendium. Each ## is a theme, each ### an ability. Built by tools/build-items.mjs.

## Time

### Time Skip

From King Crimson · Erase a moment; you alone move through it.

- **1:** Reaction: when an attack or effect targets you, Stride up to half your Speed; the attack resolves against your old space and misses. Creatures don't see you move. Only works against attacks you can see, and you can't attack or take reactions until your next turn. Your first two skips each combat cost no Stand Point; after that, Time Skip is a surge.
- **5:** At the start of each combat, glimpse the future: roll initiative twice and take the higher. Surge, 1 action: erase the next moment, and your stand Strikes through it. The Strike deals S damage instead of its normal damage, and the target can't use reactions against it.
- **9:** After a skip, the next creature you or your stand Strike this turn is off-guard to you.
- **13:** Skip moves you up to your full Speed and can carry one adjacent ally.
- **17:** Once per day, after an enemy rolls a d20, it rerolls and uses the lower result.

Time stays the rarest theme. Each Time ability is limited use rather than conditional, like the time stop model.

### Time Stop

From The World, Star Platinum · **Price:** limited charges; attacks you make in stopped time only land when time resumes, and you can't use two charges in the same round.

- **Charges:** you have 1 Time Stop charge, 2 at 9th level and 3 at 17th. All spent charges come back when you rest for 10 minutes (Refocusing counts). Time Stop doesn't cost Stand Points. Surge, free action: regain 1 spent charge. You can't use this in a round in which you used a charge.
- **1:** Free action (once per round), 1 charge: stop time and take 1 extra action at once while every other creature is frozen. Strikes and thrown objects made in stopped time hang in the air and hit when time resumes, and their targets are off-guard against them.
- **5:** You can use a charge as a reaction when a creature you can see starts an action; your stopped-time actions happen before its action resolves. You also gain +1 status bonus to initiative.
- **9:** Stopped time lasts 2 actions.
- **13:** You can move through enemies' spaces in stopped time, and a frozen creature can't take reactions against what you did there.
- **17:** Stopped time lasts 3 actions.

### Rewind

From Mandom · **Price:** you must hold a watch or clock in one hand and turn it back (Interact as part of the reaction).

- **1:** Reaction, surge: when a creature within 30 ft succeeds or fails at a check, it rerolls and must use the second result. Only you remember the first roll.
- **5:** You can use Rewind on your own check. When a Strike hits an ally within range, you can spend a Stand Point to turn the watch back a heartbeat: the target gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against that Strike, which can turn the hit into a miss (this uses your reaction).
- **9:** Once per day, undo a creature's last action instead: it returns to where it was, and any action it spent is refunded, but it must choose a different action.
- **13:** Rewind's range becomes 60 ft and it no longer costs a Stand Point once per 10 minutes.
- **17:** Once per day, rewind 6 seconds for your allies: each ally within 30 ft returns to the space and Hit Points it had at the start of your last turn.

### Accelerate

From Made in Heaven · **Price:** the aura speeds up everyone else's world but you, so allies in it are hit too.

- **1:** You gain a +10-ft status bonus to Speed. Once per combat, 1 action: your stand is quickened for 3 rounds; it can use the extra action only to Stride, Step or Strike. This doesn't cost a Stand Point. Your stand's first Strike each turn comes at accelerated speed: the target is off-guard against it.
- **5:** Surge, 1 action: you're quickened for 1 minute (the extra action only to Stride, Step or Strike, as haste). Every other creature in a 10-ft emanation, allies included, attempts a Will save or takes a –10-ft penalty to Speed for 1 round. Surge, 1 action: your stand Strikes at accelerated speed; the Strike deals S damage instead of its normal damage.
- **9:** The emanation is 20 ft, and creatures that fail are also off-guard to you.
- **13:** Your quickened action can be used for any action.
- **17:** Once per day, every other creature within 30 ft attempts a Will save: slowed 1 for 1 round on a failure, 1 minute on a critical failure (incapacitation).

### Loop

From Bites the Dust · **Price:** it only works through a willing ally who keeps your secret, and only one bomb between rests.

- **1:** Plant a bomb in a willing ally (it comes back when you rest for 10 minutes; Refocusing counts). The first enemy that Strikes or targets that ally takes S damage with no save, then the bomb is gone.
- **5:** Surge, 2 actions: plant a second bomb before your next rest, in the same or another willing ally (only one bomb at a time); the second bomb deals no damage, it only rewinds. When the bomb goes off, everyone within 30 ft except you returns to the space they held at the start of the round.
- **9:** You can choose whether the bomb triggers when it's set off.
- **13:** The ally can reroll one save or check against the triggering creature's effect, keeping the better result.
- **17:** When the bomb goes off, the triggering creature loses its remaining actions this turn, and it starts its next turn slowed 1.

## Space

### Zippers

From Sticky Fingers · Put a zipper on anything and open it.

- **1:** 1 action: stand places a zipper on a surface up to 5 ft thick and opens a passage through it until your next turn; you can also hide a light item inside a zippered surface. When your stand hits, the target attempts Fortitude: failure, it drops one held item.
- **5:** 2 actions: zip yourself along a surface, moving up to 30 ft without triggering reactions. Surge, 1 action: your stand Strikes and zips the target apart; on a hit, the Strike deals S slashing damage instead of its normal damage, and the target attempts the Fortitude save against dropping a held item.
- **9:** Reaction (once per round): when a melee attack would hit you, unzip your body so it misses. Your first two uses each combat cost no Stand Point; after that it's a surge.
- **13:** Passages work through walls up to 20 ft thick and last 1 minute.
- **17:** Critical failure on the hit save also detaches a limb: clumsy 2 and it can't use one hand for 1 minute.

### Erasure

From The Hand · **Price:** the right hand only, by swipe range.

- **1:** 1 action: your stand swipes a line of space within its reach. Choose one: pull a creature within 20 ft 10 ft toward it (Fortitude negates), or pull yourself 10 ft toward the stand.
- **5:** Surge: a swipe Strike deals S force damage instead of normal damage and ignores the target's item bonus to AC. On a miss you lose the Stand Point anyway.
- **9:** Pulls move 20 ft.
- **13:** The swipe can erase a non-magical object up to 5 ft across, or a projectile aimed at an ally within reach (reaction).
- **17:** On a critical hit with the surge Strike, the target is also drained 2.

### Void

From Cream · **Price:** you can't see or hear while inside the void, and you can only move in straight lines.

- **1:** 2 actions: you and your stand vanish into a sphere; you're undetected and can't be targeted, but you're blinded and deafened. 1 action: move up to your Speed in a straight line; creatures and objects you pass through take D force damage (basic Reflex), once per creature per turn. Your stand can become the void on its own instead, carrying it through creatures within its range while you stay outside. Leaving takes 1 action, and the void ends after 1 minute.
- **5:** Surge: passing through deals S force damage instead.
- **9:** A creature that fails its save against the void loses a piece of itself: it's drained 1, or drained 2 on a critical failure.
- **13:** You can peek out for 1 action to see for an instant without leaving the void.
- **17:** Moving inside the void costs 1 action for double your Speed.

### Dimension Step

- **5:** You can bring one willing adjacent creature. Surge: pull a creature caught between two objects into a parallel world for 1 round (Will negates). It returns at the start of your next turn. Surge: bring a parallel copy of a creature into this world; when it touches its original, both take S damage (basic Fortitude).
- **9:** A creature that critically fails its Will save against the parallel world is lost there for 1 minute.
- **13:** Range becomes 120 ft.
- **17:** No change.

### Phase Store

The next creature that touches it takes the Strike's damage (no attack roll needed; basic Reflex for half). If the stand dives into the ground, wall or gear a creature within its range is touching, the blow lands on that creature at once.
- **5:** The stored blow can be released from up to 10 ft through the object instead. Surge: store S damage instead of a Strike. A creature that fails its save against a stored blow is thrown off its footing: it's off-guard until the end of your next turn.

- **13:** Store two blows in two objects.
- **17:** A creature hit by a stored blow is also immobilized by the object's shape for 1 round (Fortitude negates).

- **5:** Strikes from a reflection gain a +1 circumstance bonus to damage per weapon damage die. Surge, 1 action: your stand strikes from the reflection in a creature's own eyes: a Strike that deals S slashing damage instead of its normal damage, the target off-guard against it.
- **9:** You can see through any reflection within 60 ft.
- **13:** Surge: your stand jumps between up to three reflections, making a Strike from each against different creatures.
- **17:** While in a reflection, your stand can only be targeted by creatures that can see that reflection.

### Photo Prison

From Atom Heart Father · **Price:** you need a photo or drawing of the target.

- **1:** 10 minutes: make a picture of a creature. While you hold it, you know the direction to that creature within 1 mile. In combat, your stand can instead snap a quick picture of a creature it can see within 30 ft as part of any Photo Prison action; a quick picture fades after 1 minute. 1 action: snap a quick picture and pin the creature to that instant (Will negates): on a failure it's off-guard until the end of your next turn, and on a critical failure it's also immobilized until the end of your next turn. Or 1 action: tear a corner of a quick picture: its creature takes D damage (basic Will) and, on a failure or critical failure, is pinned as above.
- **5:** Surge, 2 actions: tear part of the picture; the creature takes S damage (basic Will), once per day per picture.
- **9:** Surge: trap a creature in its picture (Will, incapacitation): on a failure it is immobilized for 1 round, on a critical failure for 1 minute.
- **13:** Picture range 5 miles. You can trap a picture of up to two creatures at once. Your surge tear takes 1 action, and tearing a corner affects up to two creatures in the same picture.
- **17:** You can trap a whole picture of up to four creatures at once.

### Pocket Room

Once per turn, when your stand hits a creature with a Strike, it can press the object to it and pull part of its body toward the room: the creature stays outside but is off-guard until the start of your next turn.

### Ghost Objects

From Burning Down the House · **Price:** only objects that were destroyed or lost.

## Matter and Life

### Restore

From Crazy Diamond · Fix what is broken, but never yourself.

- **1:** 1 action: stand repairs an object it touches (as a success on Repair, no tools), or mends a creature's wound it touches, healing D. Surge, 2 actions: heal a creature 2d8 + your key attribute, +1d8 per 2 levels. Can't target you or your stand. Reassemble: when your stand restores a broken object (1 action), it can fuse a creature it touches into it (Reflex): immobilized on a failure, restrained on a critical failure, for 1 round. Once per turn your stand can Reassemble the creature it hits as part of a Strike (1 action), breaking and fixing it in one blow.
- **5:** Reassemble also leaves a creature that fails off-guard for 1 minute (its joints fused wrong).
- **9:** Healing also ends one of persistent damage, enfeebled, clumsy or drained 1. Your surge heal takes 1 action.
- **13:** Surge heals two creatures. Mending a wound heals 2D.
- **17:** Once per day, restore a creature that died since your last turn to 1 HP, if its body is intact and your stand can touch it.

### Life-Giving

From Gold Experience · Pour life into objects and too much life into foes.

- **1:** 2 actions: stand turns an unattended object into a Tiny animal minion (1 action to command, lasts 1 minute, one at a time). When your stand critically hits, the target is stupefied 1 until the end of its next turn.
- **5:** Surge, 2 actions: grow a living patch onto a wound, healing 2d8 + your key attribute, +1d8 per 2 levels (can target you).
- **9:** Keep two animals; a creature that damages one of your animals takes half that damage back. **Leap In** (reaction, once per 10 minutes): when an ally adjacent to one of your animals is hit by a Strike, the animal leaps in the way: it and the ally each take half the damage (the animal's half isn't reflected).
- **13:** Your animal minions can be Small or Medium and gain your stand's Strike.
- **17:** Surge: your stand's next hit overloads the target's senses (Will): slowed 1 and stupefied 2 for 1 round, slowed 2 on a critical failure (incapacitation).
